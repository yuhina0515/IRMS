綁帶、位置、感測器質量與皮膚運動造成多少誤差？

本主題 12 筆交叉索引。

## 來源導讀

優先順序是與 IRMS 的關聯及閱讀價值，不是證據品質評分。

- **PMID-18401071** [Study of the motion artefacts of skin-mounted inertial sensors under different attachment conditions.](../sources/PMID-18401071.md) (2008；abstract) — 不同固定條件影響皮膚上感測器的機械頻寬，提供附件質量與固定方式的測試思路。
- **PMID-41569824** [A Functional Sensor-to-Segment Calibration Method Reduces the Effects of Varied Sensor Placement on Estimates of Segment Angular Excursion.](../sources/PMID-41569824.md) (2026；abstract) — 功能校準可降低佩戴位置差異的部分影響，但肢段間效果不同，也不能消除軟組織偽影。
- **PMID-30170137** [Analysis of the performance of 17 algorithms from a systematic review: Influence of sensor position, analysed variable and computational approach in gait timing estimation from IMU measurements.](../sources/PMID-30170137.md) (2018；abstract) — 多種步態事件演算法的表現受感測位置、訊號種類與運算方法影響，應按用途選擇。
- **PMID-28279850** [Accuracy and repeatability of single-pose calibration of inertial measurement units for whole-body motion analysis.](../sources/PMID-28279850.md) (2017；abstract) — 單姿勢校準比較不同姿勢與主被動擺放方式，校準再現性與軸向偏移需共同考量。
- **PMID-28366438** [Measuring joint kinematics of treadmill walking and running: Comparison between an inertial sensor based system and a camera-based system.](../sources/PMID-28366438.md) (2017；abstract) — 跑步與步行的 IMU 對光學誤差及重測表現不同，不能把低速結果直接外推至高動態。
- **PMID-28398224** [Inertial Sensor-Based Robust Gait Analysis in Non-Hospital Settings for Neurological Disorders.](../sources/PMID-28398224.md) (2017；abstract) — 非醫院情境步態分析同時驗證時空參數，並展示不同神經疾病的指標解讀案例。
- **PMID-28433867** [Validity and repeatability of inertial measurement units for measuring gait parameters.](../sources/PMID-28433867.md) (2017；abstract) — 足部與踝部佩戴在時空步態參數上有不同準確度，重測良好不等於所有擺放位置同樣準確。
- **PMID-22222615** [Quantitative assessment of pivot-shift using inertial sensors.](../sources/PMID-22222615.md) (2012；abstract) — 以慣性感測量化 pivot-shift 測試並檢查重複性；這是專業誘發測試，不等同居家角度監測。
- **PMID-20656291** [3D gait assessment in young and elderly subjects using foot-worn inertial sensors.](../sources/PMID-20656291.md) (2010；abstract) — 足部 IMU 的步態評估涵蓋轉彎、啟停與跨週期變化，提供單純穩態步行以外的測試設計。
- **PMID-18502130** [Joint kinematics estimate using wearable inertial and magnetic sensing modules.](../sources/PMID-18502130.md) (2008；abstract) — 下肢屈伸與內外旋的誤差相對 ROM 不同，提醒小幅非主要平面角度較難可靠量測。
- **PMID-16022983** [On the influence of soft tissue coverage in the determination of bone kinematics using skin markers.](../sources/PMID-16022983.md) (2005；abstract) — 皮膚標記受到軟組織覆蓋與肌肉活動影響，光學參考也需要評估自身誤差。
- **PMID-14614930** [Muscle activity reduces soft-tissue resonance at heel-strike during walking.](../sources/PMID-14614930.md) (2003；abstract) — 肌肉活動會改變著地時軟組織振動的阻尼，支持把人體固定與組織運動列入訊號誤差來源。
