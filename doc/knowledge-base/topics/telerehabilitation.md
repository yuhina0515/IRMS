## 來源導讀

優先順序是與 IRMS 的關聯及閱讀價值，不是證據品質評分。

- **PMID-31743238** [Effects of Virtual Exercise Rehabilitation In-Home Therapy Compared with Traditional Care After Total Knee Arthroplasty: VERITAS, a Randomized Controlled Trial.](../sources/PMID-31743238.md) (2020；abstract) — VERITAS 比較具臨床監督的居家虛擬復健與傳統照護，結果不可歸因於感測器單一元件。
- **PMID-31679511** [Technology-assisted rehabilitation following total knee or hip replacement for people with osteoarthritis: a systematic review and meta-analysis.](../sources/PMID-31679511.md) (2019；abstract) — 技術輔助置換復健的部分統計改善幅度太小，未必達到臨床重要性；髖部證據也較有限。
- **PMID-26178888** [In-Home Telerehabilitation Compared with Face-to-Face Rehabilitation After Total Knee Arthroplasty: A Noninferiority Randomized Controlled Trial.](../sources/PMID-26178888.md) (2015；abstract) — 膝置換後的非劣性試驗支持居家遠距服務；非劣性邊界與完整療程是解讀前提。
- **PMID-41188876** [Smart device-assisted telerehabilitation versus conventional rehabilitation after total nee arthroplasty: a systematic review and meta-analysis.](../sources/PMID-41188876.md) (2025；abstract) — 新近膝置換遠距統合分析呈現整體可比較結果及部分介入差異，仍需保留技術與療程異質性。
- **PMID-41374487** [A Sensor-Augmented Telerehabilitation System for Knee Osteoarthritis: A Randomized Controlled Trial of Neuromuscular, Functional, and Psychosocial Outcomes.](../sources/PMID-41374487.md) (2025；abstract) — KneE-PAD 的小型隨機研究有正向結果，完整感測回饋療程仍需更大及更長期試驗確認。
- **PMID-34199913** [Effects of Preoperative Telerehabilitation on Muscle Strength, Range of Motion, and Functional Outcomes in Candidates for Total Knee Arthroplasty: A Single-Blind Randomized Controlled Trial.](../sources/PMID-34199913.md) (2021；abstract) — 膝置換前遠距預復健試驗觀察力量、ROM 與功能；特定樣本與時程限制外推。
- **PMID-31728379** [Rehabilitation protocols following total knee arthroplasty: a review of study designs and outcome measures.](../sources/PMID-31728379.md) (2019；abstract) — 膝置換復健研究對常規療程、時程與強度的描述不足，報告應明確定義比較條件。
- **PMID-28027679** [The comparison of telerehabilitation and face-to-face rehabilitation after total knee arthroplasty: A systematic review and meta-analysis.](../sources/PMID-28027679.md) (2018；abstract) — 膝置換遠距與面對面療程統合分析比較疼痛與功能，需注意原始試驗與介入差異。
- **PMID-30269807** [Wearable Movement Sensors for Rehabilitation: A Focused Review of Technological and Clinical Advances.](../sources/PMID-30269807.md) (2018；abstract) — 聚焦回顧穿戴動作感測的工程與臨床應用，提供多族群用途與發展背景。
- **PMID-26843466** [Role of telerehabilitation in patients following total knee arthroplasty: Evidence from a systematic literature review and meta-analysis.](../sources/PMID-26843466.md) (2017；abstract) — 膝置換後遠距復健統合研究支持部分結果與面對面服務相近，介入形式需逐項比對。
- **PMID-26940798** [Clinical outcomes of remote asynchronous telerehabilitation are equivalent to traditional therapy following total knee arthroplasty: A randomized control study.](../sources/PMID-26940798.md) (2017；abstract) — 非同步遠距服務的隨機研究比較功能、資源使用與滿意度，呈現不同於視訊的模式。
- **PMID-27529575** [Patient Satisfaction with In-Home Telerehabilitation After Total Knee Arthroplasty: Results from a Randomized Controlled Trial.](../sources/PMID-27529575.md) (2017；abstract) — 膝置換隨機試驗的滿意度分析提供服務可接受性，滿意度不能替代功能或安全結果。
- **PMID-28455277** [Evidence of Benefit of Telerehabitation After Orthopedic Surgery: A Systematic Review.](../sources/PMID-28455277.md) (2017；abstract) — 骨科術後遠距復健回顧指出證據仍需更完整，適合說明服務模式的研究缺口。
- **PMID-25840501** [Cost analysis of in-home telerehabilitation for post-knee arthroplasty.](../sources/PMID-25840501.md) (2015；abstract) — 以膝置換後服務模式分析居家遠距成本，成本結論依賴所在地人力、交通與組織安排。
- **PMID-23474735** [Effectiveness of an interactive virtual telerehabilitation system in patients after total knee arthoplasty: a randomized controlled trial.](../sources/PMID-23474735.md) (2013；abstract) — 膝置換後短期互動式遠距復健試驗提供特定程序的比較結果，不能外推所有居家軟體。
- **PMID-21248209** [Internet-based outpatient telerehabilitation for patients following total knee arthroplasty: a randomized controlled trial.](../sources/PMID-21248209.md) (2011；abstract) — 膝置換後的網路遠距復健隨機試驗比較療程後結果，支持特定服務模式的可行性。
- **PMID-21398389** [A randomized controlled trial of home telerehabilitation for post-knee arthroplasty.](../sources/PMID-21398389.md) (2011；abstract) — 居家膝置換復健試驗呈現整體可比較結果，部分追蹤指標仍偏向常規照護。
- **PMID-21492030** [Patients' satisfaction of healthcare services and perception with in-home telerehabilitation and physiotherapists' satisfaction toward technology for post-knee arthroplasty: an embedded study in a randomized trial.](../sources/PMID-21492030.md) (2011；abstract) — 嵌入隨機試驗的滿意度研究同時評估患者與治療師，適合補足量測性能之外的接受度。
